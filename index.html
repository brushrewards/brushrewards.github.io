Why we built it

Rewards are the motivation.
Better oral health is the goal.

Knowing you should brush your teeth doesn't always make it easy to stay consistent. Brush Rewards is being built to give people an extra reason to maintain that daily habit.

By connecting consistent brushing with positive reinforcement and rewards, we want to make good oral-care habits more motivating and easier to maintain.

Our aim is simple: encourage people to build and maintain a consistent brushing habit.

Brush Rewards is not a replacement for dental care, regular check-ups or professional advice. It does not diagnose, treat or claim to prevent dental conditions.
